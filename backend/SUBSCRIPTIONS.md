# Recurring quote/order subscriptions

A quote is only a proposal. Conversion to an order copies `is_subscription`, `billing_interval`, and `billing_start_date`; only an order in `open`/`in_progress` produces invoices. A subscription requires a tenant-owned customer, a start date, and one of `weekly`, `monthly`, `quarterly`, `semiannual`, `yearly`. Existing maintenance contracts and WaaS billing remain separate. Non-subscription documents retain their previous behavior and defaults.

Dates are UTC calendar dates. Billing begins on the start date, then weekly every seven days or at integral multiples of 1/3/6/12 calendar months from the *original anchor*. Day 29/30/31 clips to the last day of short months, then returns to the original day when available (e.g. January 31 → February 29 in a leap year → March 31). A draft is generated at each period start. The scheduler catches up missed periods on startup and then wakes on each UTC midnight; it runs alongside the backend server in the Docker entrypoint, without a user request. Errors are logged to stderr and a failed order is retried at the next run/restart. Non-Docker deployments must launch `dart run bin/order_subscription_scheduler.dart` alongside the backend after migrations.

The order row is locked and `(order_id, period_start)` is a primary key. Each cycle copies all current order items (group labels and price/tax snapshots) without `order_item_id`, so the recurring cycle does not consume one-off item markers; non-subscription partial invoicing remains unchanged. The invoice notes identify the exact billing period and retain the order notes. The generated invoice remains `draft` and invisible in the customer portal; nothing sends or charges it. The manual order-to-invoice action is blocked for subscriptions to prevent duplicate billing; separate invoices can still cover exceptional services. Missing customer/items and cancelled/completed orders are skipped. Changing cadence/start does not alter historical invoices and only creates periods *after* the latest generated period; skipped historical gaps are deliberately not backfilled after a cadence change. Item edits affect future drafts only. Do not manually delete a generated invoice or its order: restrictive FKs protect the audit trail.
